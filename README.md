## About me

I'm an M.S. Computer Science student at **Columbia University** (Machine Learning track, Fall 2026 to Dec 2027). Before that I did my B.Tech at **VIT Vellore** in CSE (Blockchain Technology), finishing with a 9.15 CGPA and ranked 6th in my program.

My research is about the parts of ML that break quietly: models that change correct answers when pushed, synthetic data that bakes in bias, and training loops that collapse on their own outputs.

## Experience

**AI Research Intern** · Jio Institute, Navi Mumbai · *Jun 2026 to Jul 2026*
- Studied self-consistent deceptive behavior in LLMs under multi-turn adversarial probing, using a Solver–Prober setup across different prompting tones
- Built automated experiment pipelines with LangChain, LangGraph and the OpenRouter API, running controlled evaluations on GSM8K and Visual7W
- Extended MCQ option-elimination experiments to vision-language models by building visual reasoning benchmarks

**Undergraduate Research Assistant** · VIT Vellore · Advisor: Prof. Gopichand G · *Jul 2025 to May 2026*
- Research on bias mitigation and synthetic data quality, focused on LLM-guided augmentation
- Designed experiments measuring fairness and subgroup performance gaps; contributed problem formulation and statistical analysis to an IEEE paper
